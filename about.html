Who We Are

More Than a Pharmacy —
A Healthcare Partner

Stackly was born in 2018 with a single mission: make quality healthcare accessible to every household in Salem and beyond. We started as a small counter at MMR Complex and have grown into Tamil Nadu's most loved online pharmacy.

Every order we fulfil represents a person who trusts us with their health. That responsibility drives everything we do — from sourcing only genuine, temperature-controlled medicines to ensuring our pharmacists are available round the clock to answer your questions.
